The Saved Library

Everything you saved on Instagram, sorted onto shelves

Three steps: one bookmark reads your saved posts, and your AI tool builds them into a library with search and a list of what's worth doing.

🔒
Read-only. No likes, no saves, no unsaves and no changes to your account. The file downloads only to your computer.

  1. Drag the button to your bookmarks bar

    Click and hold the blue button, then drag it to the bookmarks bar under the address bar.

    No bookmarks bar? Ctrl + Shift + B, on a Mac ⌘ + Shift + B. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox, on a computer only.

    Safari? Dragging doesn't always work there. The easiest fix is to open this page in Chrome.

  2. Open Instagram and click the bookmark

    Open instagram.com while logged in, and click "My saved posts" in the bar. A small box at the top counts as it reads, about a minute per 100 saved posts. When it says "Done", a file named instagram-saved-....json lands in your Downloads folder.

  3. Let your AI tool build the library

    I have Claude Code

    Open Claude Code and paste the line. It installs the skill and starts. With the Claude in Chrome extension you can even skip the bookmark: it reads by itself.

    I have Codex

    Open Codex and paste the line. It finds the file in your Downloads folder. If it asks for permission, approve.

    I only have ChatGPT

    Start a new chat, attach the downloaded file and saved-library-chatgpt.py from the chatgpt folder next to this page, and paste the prompt.

At the end a page opens on your computer: a wall of all your saved posts breaks apart and flies onto shelves by topic. Summaries are written by AI and may be wrong.
